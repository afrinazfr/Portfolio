01 About

Keeping projects documented, on schedule and visible to the people who decide.

I studied Business Management at Universitas Bina Nusantara and started in project delivery during my degree, as a PMO intern on the Telkomsel Digital World project at PT Phincon. There I supported two Scrum teams through planning, tracking and documentation.

Roles in account management and campaign strategy followed, where I learned to work from client objectives and market data. I then returned to IT projects as a Project Management Officer at PT Mandiri Utama Finance.

My work now sits between governance and tooling: making sure documentation and timelines hold up, then improving Jira, eazyBI and dashboards when the process needs more than they offer. I'm especially interested in digital product development and business process improvement, and I'm beginning to explore product strategy.
