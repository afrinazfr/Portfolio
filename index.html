01 About

Keeping projects documented, organized dan moving forward

I studied Business Management at Universitas Bina Nusantara and started in project delivery during my degree, as a PMO intern on the Telkomsel Digital World project at PT Phincon. There I supported two Scrum teams through planning, tracking and documentation.

Digital marketing and campaign strategy roles followed, where I learned to work from client objectives, performance reports and market research. I then returned to IT projects as a Project Management Officer at PT Mandiri Utama Finance.

My work sits at the intersection of governance, technology, and business processes. I focus on keeping documentation and timelines on track while improving the tools and workflows behind them through Jira, eazyBI, and dashboards. I’m always interested in exploring how technology, business needs, and better ways of working can come together to create meaningful improvements.
